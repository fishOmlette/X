\section{Evidence-Coverage Map}
\label{app:evidence-map}

\Cref{tab:survey-matrix} provides a qualitative coding of the principal approach
classes.  ``E'' marks an obligation that is explicit and central in the cited
mechanisms; ``P'' marks mixed, conditional, or model-internal treatment; and
``--'' marks a property outside the stated mechanism.  For a row containing
several sources, mixed coverage is coded ``P''.  The columns denote semantic
intent, physical validity, temporal validity, uncertainty, compatibility,
lifecycle, and recovery.  The coding describes the cited mechanism, not every
capability of the surrounding system.  The matrix is a coverage aid, not a
ranking or frequency estimate.
