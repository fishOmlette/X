\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Predictive runtime verification and enforcement.}
Predictive runtime verification can use a priori system knowledge to produce a conclusive verdict before the observed execution alone permits one~\cite{pinisetty_rv}. Predictive runtime enforcement reduces to non-predictive enforcement when no system knowledge is available~\cite{pinisetty_re}. For stochastic linear systems, Yoon et al.~\cite{sankar_prm} determine whether a control sequence exists over a finite horizon that maintains a safety property with sufficiently high probability. RADICS combines a black-box monitor that switches to a safe algorithm on a failing trajectory with a white-box monitor that predicts such trajectories earlier~\cite{radics}. Predictive safety filters instead solve an online finite-horizon problem to certify or modify a proposed input under model uncertainty~\cite{predictive_filter}. We ask a narrower comparative question: once recovery timing and a tuned current-margin threshold are included, when does an earlier trigger change the measured outcome?

\paragraph{Mission-level runtime assurance.}
Successor-safe monitors can reject commands predicted to violate mission feasibility before execution~\cite{missionrta_ad}. Compositional monitoring can identify policy violations that emerge across a robot swarm~\cite{swarmrta}, while collective run-time adaptation addresses safety and mission-completion problems in multi-robot systems~\cite{collective2019}. Simplex motivates using a simple, reliable core to control more complex functionality~\cite{simplex}. Its black-box extension uses runtime checks to transfer control between advanced and baseline controllers and includes a multi-robot case study~\cite{blackboxsimplex}; SOTER composes robotics RTA modules that switch between advanced and safe controllers~\cite{soter}. Our comparison concerns the trigger rule: projected time-to-threshold versus fixed current-margin thresholds.

\paragraph{Adaptive safety margins.}
Adaptive margins can scale a safety buffer using relative speed, braking capability, and uncertainty~\cite{raam}; control barrier functions provide a broader framework for encoding and enforcing safety constraints~\cite{cbf}. Our reduction makes explicit that the horizon trigger studied here is one particular rate-scaled margin. This motivates comparing it with tuned fixed margins rather than only an unpadded threshold.

\paragraph{Prognostics and recoverability.}
Prognostics estimates whether and when faults may occur and informs condition-based maintenance decisions~\cite{jardine}. Runtime-assurance verification formalizes decision-timing adequacy and time-bounded recoverability~\cite{recoverproofs}. Recent adaptive-governance work fits a first-order estimate of viability-index boundary-crossing time when the fitted slope is negative and frames it, under a local-linearity assumption, as a surrogate for viability-set exit~\cite{govern}. These neighboring results motivate a point-of-no-return evaluation, but they do not by themselves establish the empirical boundary claimed for the robot model studied here.
